Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

PDF Gremza is a free, open source PDF reader and editor for macOS and Windows. It does not collect, store or send any personal data.

Your documents

PDFs are opened, edited, saved and exported only on your computer. Their content is never uploaded anywhere.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reports.

Update check

The only connection the app makes is to GitHub, to see whether a newer version exists and, if you agree, to download it:

  • It reads the latest release of Gremza/pdf-reader-free through the GitHub API, at launch and once a day.
  • No information about you or your files is sent. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • You can turn it off: uncheck Check for Updates Automatically (in the app menu on macOS, under the Updates button on Windows). Check for Updates… then only runs when you choose it.

Settings

Your update settings are stored locally on your computer and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
